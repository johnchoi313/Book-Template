# The Gate

The gate knew her number before she did.

She held out her wrist anyway. The light passed over the mark, found it, and the turnstile opened with the same tired click it gave everyone. On the far side the street was already moving: shifts changing, heads down, the speakers on the corners starting up again.

She did not look at the camera. *Looking was a way to be remembered.*

* * *

Three blocks on, the count began. A voice from a box on a pole, reading numbers that were not hers yet. She walked under it and kept walking.

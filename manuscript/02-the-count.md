# The Count

They posted the count at dusk, on the wall where the paint had been scraped back to brick.

Her number was not on it. She read the list twice to be sure, then once more because being sure had been wrong before. Behind her, someone started to run. Nobody told them to stop.

* * *

After dark the speakers went quiet. That was the only kindness the city still offered, and it was not meant as one.
